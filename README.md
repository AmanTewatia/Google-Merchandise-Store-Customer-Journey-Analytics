# Google Merchandise Store Customer Journey Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer journeys, marketing campaign performance, conversion behaviour, and revenue generation using the Google Merchandise Store dataset.

The objective is to transform raw customer journey data into actionable business insights that support executive decision-making, improve marketing effectiveness, optimise customer conversion, and increase business performance.

---

# Business Problem

Marketing teams invest across multiple acquisition channels, but not every customer successfully converts into a purchase.

This project answers key business questions including:

- Which marketing campaigns generate the highest revenue?
- Which customer journeys achieve the highest conversion rates?
- How many touchpoints are required before conversion?
- Which campaigns deliver the highest business value?
- Where should marketing investment be prioritised?

---

# Business Objectives

- Analyse customer journey performance
- Evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness
- Measure conversion performance
- Identify revenue growth opportunities
- Develop executive KPI reporting
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

# Business Insights

The project identified significant differences in customer journey performance across marketing campaigns.

Key findings include:

- High-performing campaigns generate a disproportionate share of revenue.
- Shorter customer journeys generally achieve faster conversions.
- Revenue concentration highlights opportunities for targeted marketing investment.
- Customer journey optimisation can improve overall conversion efficiency.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing campaigns.
- Optimise customer journeys with excessive touchpoints.
- Improve campaign targeting using conversion insights.
- Monitor executive KPIs through interactive dashboards.
- Support strategic marketing decisions using business intelligence.
