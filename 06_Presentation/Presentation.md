# Executive Summary

## Project

Google Merchandise Store Customer Journey Analytics

---

## Objective

To analyse customer journeys, campaign performance, conversion behaviour, and revenue generation in order to identify opportunities for improving marketing effectiveness and business performance.

---

## Tools Used

- SQL
- Power Query
- Power BI
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

---

## Business Areas Covered

- Customer Journey Analysis
- Marketing Campaign Analysis
- Revenue Analysis
- Conversion Analysis
- Executive KPI Reporting

---

## Key Deliverables

- Data Cleaning & Preparation
- SQL Analysis
- Executive Dashboard
- Business Insights
- Strategic Recommendations

---

## Business Outcome

The project demonstrates how business intelligence techniques can transform raw customer journey data into actionable insights that support marketing optimisation, revenue growth, and executive decision-making.
