# Business Understanding

## Project Overview

The Google Merchandise Store is an official e-commerce platform that sells Google-branded products such as clothing, accessories, office supplies, and lifestyle merchandise.

This project analyses customer behaviour, website traffic, marketing performance, product sales, and revenue generation to identify business opportunities that improve customer acquisition, increase conversions, and maximize revenue.

---

## Business Problem

Although the store attracts visitors from multiple marketing channels, not every visitor completes a purchase.

The business wants to understand:

- Which marketing channels generate the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most sales?
- Where customers drop off during the purchase journey?
- Which customer segments convert better?
- What factors influence revenue growth?

The objective is to transform raw transactional and customer interaction data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- Evaluate marketing channel effectiveness.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Measure conversion performance across customer journeys.
- Analyse revenue trends and purchasing patterns.
- Build an executive dashboard for business decision-making.
- Recommend strategies to improve sales and customer retention.
