# Business Questions

The following business questions guide the analysis and form the basis for SQL queries, Power BI dashboards, and business recommendations.

## Customer Analysis

1. How many unique customers visited the store?
2. What percentage of visitors completed a purchase?
3. What is the ratio of new vs returning customers?
4. Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?

---

## Revenue Analysis

5. What is the total revenue generated?
6. What is the monthly revenue trend?
7. Which months recorded the highest and lowest sales?
8. What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?

---

## Product Performance

9. Which products generated the highest revenue?
10. Which products were purchased most frequently?
11. Which product categories perform best?
12. Which products contribute the least revenue?

---

## Marketing Performance

13. Which marketing channels drive the highest traffic?
14. Which traffic sources generate the highest revenue?
15. Which acquisition channels have the highest conversion rate?

---

## Customer Journey

16. Where do customers drop off before making a purchase?
17. Which pages are most commonly viewed before conversion?
18. Which customer paths lead to the highest purchase probability?

---

## Business Performance

19. Which KPIs should executives monitor regularly?
20. What recommendations can increase revenue and improve customer retention?
