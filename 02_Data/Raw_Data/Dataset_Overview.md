# Dataset Overview

## Dataset Name

Google Merchandise Store Customer Journey Dataset

---

## Dataset Source

Kaggle – Ecommerce User Journeys (Google Merchandise Store)

---

## Dataset Description

The dataset captures customer journey interactions within the Google Merchandise Store. It contains marketing campaign information, customer touchpoints, conversion performance, revenue generated, and the time taken for customers to complete key business events.

The data enables the analysis of customer behaviour, campaign effectiveness, conversion funnels, and revenue performance, supporting data-driven decision-making for marketing and business teams.

To analyse customer journeys, campaign performance, conversion behaviour, and revenue trends in order to identify business opportunities, improve marketing effectiveness, and support executive decision-making.
