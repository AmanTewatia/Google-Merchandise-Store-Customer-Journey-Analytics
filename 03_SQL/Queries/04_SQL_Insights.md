# SQL Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The SQL analysis examined customer journey behaviour, marketing campaign effectiveness, conversion performance, and revenue generation across the Google Merchandise Store. The analysis identified key business drivers influencing customer conversion and overall business performance.

---

# Key Findings

## Customer Journey

- Customer journeys varied considerably in length, indicating differences in purchasing behaviour.
- Shorter customer journeys generally resulted in faster conversions.
- Higher numbers of touchpoints did not always translate into higher revenue, suggesting diminishing returns beyond a certain engagement level.

---

## Marketing Campaign Performance

- A small number of campaigns contributed a disproportionately large share of total revenue.
- High-performing campaigns demonstrated both strong conversion rates and shorter customer journeys.
- Several campaigns generated traffic but produced relatively low conversion performance, highlighting optimisation opportunities.

---

## Revenue Performance

- Revenue distribution was highly concentrated among the top-performing customer journeys.
- Average revenue per converted journey provides an effective benchmark for evaluating future marketing initiatives.
- Revenue analysis identified campaigns with strong commercial performance that can be prioritised for future investment.

---

## Conversion Analysis

- Conversion rates differed significantly across campaigns.
- Campaigns requiring fewer customer touchpoints generally achieved higher conversion efficiency.
- Longer conversion cycles indicate opportunities to simplify the customer purchase journey.

---

## Business Opportunities

The analysis suggests opportunities to:

- Increase investment in high-performing campaigns.
- Optimise underperforming marketing channels.
- Reduce unnecessary customer journey steps.
- Improve conversion efficiency through better campaign targeting.
- Allocate marketing budgets based on revenue contribution rather than traffic volume alone.

---

# Business Value

This SQL analysis enables business stakeholders to:

- Monitor campaign performance.
- Evaluate customer journey effectiveness.
- Identify revenue growth opportunities.
- Improve marketing return on investment (ROI).
- Support strategic business decision-making using data.
