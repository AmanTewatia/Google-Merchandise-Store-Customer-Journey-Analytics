# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed.

---

## Marketing Optimisation

- Increase investment in high-performing campaigns.
- Review and optimise campaigns with low conversion efficiency.
- Allocate budgets based on revenue contribution rather than traffic volume.

---

## Customer Journey Optimisation

- Reduce unnecessary customer touchpoints.
- Simplify the customer purchase journey.
- Improve user experience during key conversion stages.

---

## Revenue Growth

- Prioritise campaigns generating the highest return on investment.
- Expand successful customer acquisition strategies.
- Monitor campaign performance using executive dashboards.

---

## Business Intelligence

- Implement real-time KPI monitoring.
- Track conversion trends regularly.
- Develop automated reporting to support management decisions.

---

## Future Improvements

Future versions of the project could include:

- Customer segmentation.
- Predictive conversion modelling.
- Marketing attribution modelling.
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis.
- Churn prediction.
