# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The analysis of the Google Merchandise Store customer journey dataset identified key trends in customer behaviour, marketing performance, conversion efficiency, and revenue generation. The findings highlight opportunities to optimise marketing investments, improve customer conversion, and increase overall business performance.

---

# Customer Journey Insights

- Customer journeys differ significantly in terms of touchpoints and conversion time.
- Shorter customer journeys generally achieve faster conversions.
- Additional customer interactions do not always increase conversion probability.

---

# Campaign Performance Insights

- Marketing campaign performance varies considerably.
- A small number of campaigns contribute a significant share of total revenue.
- High-converting campaigns demonstrate better customer acquisition efficiency.

---

# Revenue Insights

- Revenue is concentrated among a limited number of customer journeys.
- Average revenue per converted customer provides a useful benchmark for campaign evaluation.
- Revenue contribution varies significantly across campaigns.

---

# Conversion Insights

- Conversion performance differs across marketing campaigns.
- Campaigns with fewer customer touchpoints generally convert more efficiently.
- Longer customer journeys present optimisation opportunities.

---

# Business Impact

The analysis enables stakeholders to:

- Improve marketing budget allocation.
- Increase campaign efficiency.
- Optimise customer journeys.
- Improve conversion performance.
- Support strategic business planning using data-driven insights.
