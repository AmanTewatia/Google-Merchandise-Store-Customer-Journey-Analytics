# DAX Measures

The dashboard uses calculated measures to support executive reporting and KPI tracking.

## Revenue KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Average Revenue
- Revenue by Campaign

## Customer KPIs

- Total Customer Journeys
- Average Touchpoints
- Average Days till Conversion

## Marketing KPIs

- Average Conversion Rate
- Campaign Performance
- Campaign Revenue Contribution

## Executive KPIs

- Revenue Growth
- Conversion Performance
- Campaign Effectiveness
